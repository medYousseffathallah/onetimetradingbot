What deserves attention

The fixture illustrates a sparse-market question: recent observed prices have stabilized after a decline, but trading activity remains episodic.

  • Price behavior is a research lead, not proof of a catalyst.
  • Missing sessions cannot be treated as zero returns.
  • Fundamental quality cannot be scored without dated statements.
